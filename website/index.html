  1. The diary opens at nightBetween 20:00 and 05:00, your page for the night is open.
  2. Write one pageOne photo and a few words, just for tonight.
  3. Send it to friends, like a letterChoose who gets it. Once it is sent, it is sent. Nobody sees who else received it, and there are no read receipts.
  4. Letters arrive the next eveningAt 20:00, last night’s letters from your friends arrive, ready to read.
  5. Then they fadeLetters disappear when the night ends at 05:00. Your own pages stay in your diary.
